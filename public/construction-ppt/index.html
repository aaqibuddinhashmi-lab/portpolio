04 DEFINE

Framing the Core Challenge

How Might We

Help construction teams monitor progress, reduce waste, and verify plans — without adding complexity?

⚠️

Reactive, Not Proactive

Construction sites react to problems after they occur. No early warning systems exist.

👁️

Poor Visibility

Progress tracking relies on manual site visits and verbal updates. Data is fragmented.

🗑️

Invisible Waste

Material waste goes unnoticed until financial loss accumulates. No measurement exists.

📋

Expert-Dependent Compliance

IS Code verification requires experienced professionals — creating bottlenecks and errors.

🔌

Disconnected Clients

Homeowners feel excluded from their own project. No simple way to track progress.

🧩

Fragmented Tools

Teams juggle WhatsApp, Excel, calls, and paper. No unified platform exists for Indian builders.

09 KEY FEATURES

Intelligent by Design

FEATURE 01

Drone Monitoring System

Capture daily site imagery automatically. BuildIQ compares planned vs. actual progress, detects unsafe zones, and generates heatmaps of inactivity areas.

Live Feed Progress Heatmap Safety Alerts
FEATURE 02

Material Waste Tracking

Stop financial bleed before it happens. AI generates end-of-day smart reports tracking cement, steel, sand, and wood—translating waste percentages into real monetary costs.

FEATURE 03

Smart Project Dashboard

The command center for your site. See project completion, delay risks, budget utilization, and live site feeds in one unified, scannable interface.

FEATURE 04

AI Plan Verification

Upload AutoCAD or PDF plans. The AI instantly compares them against Indian Standard (IS) Codes for structural spacing, ventilation, safety, and fire exits.
